# v1.1.1 — Full Mod bug-fix update

Release date: 2026-09-15.

This release updates Full Mod only. Stock + LDAC Fix is unchanged from v1.1.0.

## Full Mod fixes

- Restored the exact official v1.3 music listview, removing the Full Mod album-opening latency.
- Restored the stock long-press Power shutdown countdown/overlay. This restores the visual countdown behavior without changing shutdown timing or power management.
- Corrected Balance Quick Settings artwork with the native Balance glyph, modern circular styling, correct transparency and the original smaller glyph size.

The LDAC decoder correction, PEQ, Bluetooth components, kernel, radio firmware and updater/recovery are unchanged.

## Upstream / device limitations

The following behaviors are also present in official firmware or appear to be device-level limitations; they are not introduced by Full Mod.

- Album-art screensaver current-track information/artwork may take about 1.5 seconds to update after a track change. The same behavior was reproduced on official TempoTec v1.2 and v1.3 firmware. Changing `tf_image_cache_enable` did not eliminate it.
- Bluetooth reception has limited link margin, particularly with sustained high-bitrate LDAC and congested RF conditions. Lower-rate LDAC can also become unstable in difficult conditions, while AAC has been more reliable in observed normal use. This is separate from the v1.3 LDAC decoder corruption corrected by this project; the exact RF-level cause remains unconfirmed.

See [BLUETOOTH-RANGE.md](../BLUETOOTH-RANGE.md) for the detailed investigation.
