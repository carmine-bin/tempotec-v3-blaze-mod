# v1.1.0 — official TempoTec v1.3 generation

Release date: 2026-09-12.

**Stock + LDAC Fix**

Official TempoTec v1.3 appearance and features, including PEQ and TempoTec's stability fixes, plus this project's hardware-confirmed fix for the v1.3 LDAC digital corruption regression.

**Full Mod**

The same corrected v1.3 base, plus the complete modern HiBy-style UI port and all validated V3 Blaze customizations from the previous mod, with final v1.3 layout corrections. Its tested pull-down is brightness-only; historical claims of a visible volume slider were inaccurate.

## Separate unresolved issue

Bluetooth range/interference remains under investigation. Higher LDAC rates are unreliable, and lower rates can still cut in crowded RF environments. AAC remains stable in the observed normal-use scenarios. Neither firmware claims to fix range; this is separate from corrected decoder corruption. Wi-Fi range also appears poor; underlying cause unknown.

Both editions have been tested on physical V3 Blaze hardware. Stock Fix is the exact TEST 2 UPT; Full Mod is the exact successful final UI-state port. No rebuilt substitute is used. Official v1.3 provides PEQ, real-time Bluetooth search and stability/reboot improvements. The project independently corrects the decoder's downstream coefficient gate; its author/purpose remain unknown. Full Mod's player patch is independently relocated to v1.3.

## Downloads and installation

- `V3-Blaze-v1.3-Stock-LDAC-Fix.upt`
- `V3-Blaze-v1.3-Full-Mod.upt`
- `SHA256SUMS`, `MD5SUMS`, `release-manifest.json`

Verify checksums, **rename the chosen image to `v3_analog_2025.upt`**, place it at the microSD root and use the normal updater. Read [installation](../INSTALL.md) and [recovery](../RECOVERY.md). Never leave a spare `update.upt`, which recovery prioritizes.
