# V3 Blaze Firmware v1.2.2

## Full Mod: last track of an album

- In play-in-order mode, playing the last track of an album stopped it after about a second, and selecting it again started the album's first track. Fixed.
- Cause: Full Mod enables the TF image cache, which makes Now Playing look ahead to the next track to prefetch its cover. On the last track, that look-ahead ran the player's end-of-list stop. The look-ahead is removed; covers are still cached, each one when its track plays.

Tested on a physical V3 Blaze; the released image is byte-identical to the tested one.

Stock + Fixes is unchanged from v1.2.0 and was not affected.

## Known limitations

The pull-down menu's battery icon keeps its white frame at low charge.

The album-art screensaver may take about 1.5 seconds to update after a track change; the same behavior occurs on official TempoTec v1.2 and v1.3.

Select the desired firmware and rename it to `v3_analog_2025.upt` for microSD installation.
