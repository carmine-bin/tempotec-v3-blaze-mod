# V3 Blaze Firmware v1.2.1

## Full Mod: low-battery indicator

- Below 16 %, the battery icon used to turn solid red and hide the remaining level. The level now keeps the theme color, and the battery frame turns red instead, as on the official firmware.
- The low-battery notice showed an empty grey card. It now shows its message.
- The battery frame's corners and cap are drawn solid.

Both changes were tested on a physical V3 Blaze; the released image is byte-identical to the tested one.

Stock + Fixes is unchanged from v1.2.0 and carries the same Bluetooth Receiver corrections.

## Known limitations

The pull-down menu's battery icon keeps its white frame at low charge.

The album-art screensaver may take about 1.5 seconds to update after a track change; the same behavior occurs on official TempoTec v1.2 and v1.3.

Select the desired firmware and rename it to `v3_analog_2025.upt` for microSD installation.
