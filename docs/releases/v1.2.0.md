# V3 Blaze Firmware v1.2.0

## Bluetooth Receiver dropouts fixed

In official v1.3, BlueZ kept the Bluetooth radio busy with LE background scanning and fast-connectable page scanning while the Blaze was receiving audio. The source could not sustain LDAC 990 kb/s even at close range: in testing, the phone discarded about 60 % of the audio next to the Blaze.

Both editions now set `ControllerMode = bredr` and `FastConnectable = false` in `/etc/bluetooth/main.conf`. LDAC 990 kb/s plays with no dropped audio at close and moderate range, and holds up to about 12 m through two doors, where the official configuration failed before the first door.

The Blaze no longer uses Bluetooth LE; no feature that needs it was found. Incoming connections can take up to about a second longer to start.

[Cause, measurements and what did not help](https://github.com/carmine-bin/tempotec-v3-blaze-mod/blob/main/docs/BLUETOOTH-RANGE.md)

## Editions

- **Stock + Fixes** (formerly Stock + LDAC Fix): official v1.3 with the LDAC decoder correction and the Bluetooth configuration change.
- **Full Mod**: the same corrections; interface and playback unchanged from v1.1.2.

## Known limitations

The album-art screensaver may take about 1.5 seconds to update after a track change; the same behavior occurs on official TempoTec v1.2 and v1.3.

Beyond about 12 m with walls in between, LDAC 990 kb/s still drops out and the source lowers its rate.

Select the desired firmware and rename it to `v3_analog_2025.upt` for microSD installation.
